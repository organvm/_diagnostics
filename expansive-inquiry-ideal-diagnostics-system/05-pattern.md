# Pattern — what only the overlay reveals

The rule of this lens: report only what is visible in the *cross-lens overlay* — motifs that
appear in more than one lens, not themes restated from a single one.

## Cross-Lens Motifs

Four motifs recur across three or more lenses (the threshold for "emergent"):

1. **Negation as the path to truth** — *Logic* (quarantine the lying signal; the headline is reached by *demoting* swap-%; the delta is a difference, not an assertion), *Mythos* (the Apophatic Oracle, "inversion-toward-absence," the boon is a *subtraction*), *Bridge* (apophatic theology; "negative diagnosis" as ranked-excluded-states; taphonomy — absence as evidence). Truth is approached by removing falsehood.

2. **The conjugate pair: information vs. footprint** — *Logic* (zero-steady-state-perturbation and guaranteed-transient-capture are *conjugate*; cannot have both), *Mythos* (the healer who stays to watch closely *becomes* the fever's hunger), *Bridge* (passive vs. active sonar — the most informative ping is the most self-exposing). Three independent derivations of the *same* uncertainty relation.

3. **Asymptotic self-erasure as telos** — *Scope* ("make itself unnecessary"), *Logic* (self-abolition = duty-cycle minimization → dormant verifier), *Mythos* (Pole Star; "its telos is its own silence"), *Bridge* (the via negativa instrument "dissolves into the relation"). The ideal is a limit the system approaches by fading, not a state it occupies.

4. **Time-vintage as the founding wound** — *Logic* (`R(B,S,t)`; the staleness bound; the 48h gap), *Mythos* (coroner-vs-physician; the ghost two days dead), *Bridge* (archaeological stratigraphy and dating; central-bank lag and revision). Every lens locates the original sin in the *gap between when a thing was true and when it is acted on*.

## Three Meta-Patterns

**MP1 — Faithfulness is a subtractive achievement, not an additive one.**
*(Logic + Mythos + Bridge.)* Every lens independently rejects the intuition that better diagnosis means *more* signal. Logic ranks-and-demotes; Mythos knows by what it refuses; Bridge reports deltas and excluded states. The diagnostic instrument gets *more* faithful by admitting *less* — quarantining liars, reporting differences rather than levels, naming what is provably-not-happening. This inverts the engineering default (add sensors, fuse signals). The ideal machination is a *filter*, not an *accumulator*.

**MP2 — Observation and capture are conjugate variables.**
*(Logic + Mythos + Bridge.)* There is a hard trade-off with the structure of an uncertainty principle: you cannot simultaneously have zero resident footprint *and* guaranteed capture of an unannounced transient. All three lenses derive it from different physics (resource economics, the healer's rent, sonar emission-cost). The shared resolution is also identical: **externalize and impulse the trigger** — let the patient's own cheap ambient signal decide *when* to spend the expensive, perturbing observation. Non-perturbing *continuous* diagnosis is impossible; threshold-triggered *impulsive* diagnosis approaches the limit.

**MP3 — The objective function points away from the instrument's own use.**
*(Scope + Logic + Mythos + Bridge — all five.)* This is the deepest and most universally attested pattern, and the most teleologically strange. Almost every system optimizes for its own engagement; the ideal diagnostics system optimizes *against* it. Its success metric is its own falling duty-cycle — the dormant verifier, the Pole Star forgotten for whole nights, the via-negativa dissolving into right relation. A diagnostics system whose utility rises with the patient's sickness is misaligned by construction; the ideal aligns the observer's objective with the *health* of the observed by making every finding install the structural change that removes its own recurrence.
